## 3. What P26 will NOT do (the boundary laws carried forward)

- **No live-mode activation.** INV-25 stands. ADR-0019 defines readiness; the activation is a separate operator order with its own certification evidence.
- **No security-model weakening.** The chat-confirmation gate keeps refusing STRONG_CONFIRM/REAUTH in ordinary conversation; the model still cannot mint authorizations or re-auth proofs; the seal, the RAK, the digest binding and the single-use laws are untouched in their semantics.
- **No webhook-endpoint or app-secrets un-hold.** Those holds are the secrets-never-enter-chat law, not missing flows (the D12 host destination plane owns webhook destinations; secrets stay operator-side). The P26 re-auth flow un-holds ONLY the three account-link/session operations whose responses carry URLs/sessions, not credentials.
- **No attachment plane.** Large binary uploads remain an honest carry-forward (an architecture change, deliberately not smuggled into P26).
- **No scraping or dashboard automation.** The parity inventory is evidence-cited documentation joined to the census (ADR-0017 D13's law: honest classification, never browser automation).
